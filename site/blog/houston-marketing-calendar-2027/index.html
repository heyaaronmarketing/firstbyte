2027 Marketing Calendar for Houston and The Woodlands Businesses: Every Date Worth Planning Around

Six weeks. That’s roughly how far ahead of a big date most Houston campaigns need to start, and it’s the number almost everyone forgets. The Black Friday ad that goes live on Thanksgiving morning is bidding against people who started warming up their lists in October.

So this calendar does two things. It lists the 2027 dates we could actually confirm for Houston and The Woodlands, with a link to the source for each one. And for every month it tells you what you should be starting, which is usually a different thing from what’s happening that month.

The short version: the 2027 dates that move the most money for Houston and The Woodlands businesses are the Houston Marathon (Jan. 17), RODEOHOUSTON (March 2-21), spring break (March 15-19 in Conroe and Cy-Fair ISD), IRONMAN Texas (April 24), hurricane season (from June 1), the sales tax holiday (Aug. 6-8), the first day of school (Aug. 11 in Katy and Cy-Fair ISD) and Black Friday (Nov. 26).

Where a date wasn’t published when we wrote this, we say “typically” and give last year’s date. Check those before you print anything.

How to use this Houston calendar, and what we left out

Work backward from each date. The event is the deadline. The campaign starts weeks earlier, and the offer, the landing page and the creative have to exist before that.

We left out things we couldn’t verify for 2027, like most festival dates that organizers announce in the spring, and we skipped dates that matter to almost nobody’s marketing (sorry, Groundhog Day). We also didn’t repeat the full rodeo playbook here. That lives in our rodeo season marketing guide, and the budget side of the year is in the 2027 marketing plan for Houston small businesses. January 2027: marathon weekend and rodeo tickets

The Chevron Houston Marathon and Aramco Houston Half run on Sunday, Jan. 17, with the We Are Houston 5K on Saturday, Jan. 16, and a race weekend that runs from Jan. 13, per ABC13. Organizers expect around 40,000 participants, their most ever. That’s a lot of out-of-town runners in downtown hotels, plus their families looking for breakfast, massages, physical therapy and somewhere to eat a very large dinner on Sunday night.

What to run: for restaurants, recovery studios and PT clinics near downtown, Google Ads on “near me” searches that week, and Business Profile posts with race-weekend hours. Book it in December. By the second week of January, everyone who planned is already live.

January is also when the rodeo goes from rumor to calendar. The nightly concert lineup is announced Jan. 7 and individual tickets go on sale Jan. 14, per Fox 26. If you’re running a rodeo-night promotion, those are your two content days. And it’s resolution season, the one month a gym, a med spa or a meal-prep business doesn’t need to explain why someone should start now.

Start this month: Lunar New Year and Valentine’s creative, rodeo-season offers, and your spring break plan.

February 2027: Lunar New Year, Valentine’s, the parade

Lunar New Year falls on Saturday, Feb. 6, and 2027 is the Year of the Goat, per Royal Museums Greenwich. In Houston that’s a big weekend along Bellaire Boulevard and in Sugar Land, Katy and Pearland, where a lot of Chinese and Vietnamese American families live. Restaurants, bakeries, salons and jewelry stores are the obvious players. If you’re marketing to those audiences, language and cultural details matter more than the date itself; our post on Asian American marketing in Houston covers what we’d get right before spending a dollar.

Valentine’s Day is a Sunday, Feb. 14. A Sunday Valentine’s spreads restaurant demand across Friday and Saturday, so sell the whole weekend. Conroe ISD students are out Friday, Feb. 12 and Monday, Feb. 15 (Presidents’ Day), per Conroe ISD’s 2026-27 calendar, which makes it a four-day weekend for a lot of families in The Woodlands, Conroe and Montgomery County. Kids’ activity businesses should notice that before the week arrives.

The Downtown Rodeo Parade is Saturday, Feb. 27, per Click2Houston. Then freeze risk: Houston typically gets at least one hard freeze threat in January or February. Plumbers, HVAC companies and pool services should have a freeze landing page and a paused ad group built in December, ready to flip on the afternoon the forecast turns.

March 2027: rodeo, spring break and the Houston Open

RODEOHOUSTON runs March 2 to 21 at NRG Park, per Fox 26. For restaurants, bars, western wear, hotels and anyone near NRG, it’s the biggest three weeks of the first half. For many service businesses it’s a distraction, because your customers’ evenings are booked. The full week-by-week plan is in our rodeo season guide, so we won’t repeat it.

Spring break lands inside the last week of rodeo. Conroe ISD is out March 15 to 19, per Conroe ISD’s 2026-27 calendar, and Cy-Fair ISD has the same week, per Community Impact. Katy, Klein, Tomball, Humble and Houston ISD set their own calendars, so check yours. That week, camps, trampoline parks, pet boarding and tutoring get busy, while appointment businesses see no-shows climb. Send reminder texts two days out instead of one.

Daylight saving time starts Sunday, March 14. One more hour of evening light matters to patio restaurants, landscapers and anyone selling outdoor projects.

The Texas Children’s Houston Open is March 22 to 28 at Memorial Park, per the PGA Tour’s 2027 schedule. Easter is Sunday, March 28. Conroe ISD has student holidays on Friday, March 26 and Monday, March 29, which makes another four-day weekend north of the Grand Parkway.

Start this month: IRONMAN weekend plans if you’re in The Woodlands, and your Mother’s Day offer.

April 2027: IRONMAN Texas in The Woodlands, and tax day

Memorial Hermann IRONMAN Texas is Saturday, April 24, per IRONMAN’s 2027 schedule, via Slowtwitch. The race stays in The Woodlands through 2030 under an extension Visit The Woodlands approved in 2024, per Community Impact. Race day changes the whole town: the swim is in Lake Woodlands, the bike course heads out to the Hardy Toll Road, and the run loops around the Waterway and its trails until late at night.

That cuts both ways. Hotels, restaurants near Market Street and Hughes Landing, bike shops, massage and recovery studios get a weekend of athletes and families. A salon on Woodlands Parkway might lose half its Saturday to road closures. If you’re in the second group, tell your customers early which streets will be closed and when you’re open; it saves you a day of no-shows and confused phone calls. If you’re in the first group, run Business Profile posts and tight-radius search ads from Wednesday on, when athletes start arriving.

Tax day is Thursday, April 15, which matters for CPAs, bookkeepers and financial advisors, who should have stopped advertising for new returns weeks earlier and started advertising extensions and next-year planning.

The emergency supplies sales tax holiday typically falls on the last weekend of April. It ran April 25 to 27 in 2026, per the Comptroller’s emergency supplies page; the 2027 dates weren’t posted when we wrote this. Hardware stores, generator dealers and anyone selling batteries or storm gear should watch for the announcement. Conroe ISD students also have a holiday on Friday, April 30.

May 2027: Mother’s Day, the last day of school and storm prep

Mother’s Day is Sunday, May 9. Florists, restaurants, spas and boutiques in The Woodlands and Kingwood already know. What they often miss is that the gift-card shopper is a procrastinator. Run gift-card ads on the Thursday and Friday before, aimed at people within a few miles, with “buy online, print at home” in the headline.

The ENERGY STAR and water-efficient products tax holidays typically run over Memorial Day weekend (May 23 to 25 in 2026, per the Comptroller’s 2026 announcement). HVAC companies, appliance stores and irrigation contractors can build an offer around them, as long as the ad is specific about which items qualify.

The last day of school is Thursday, May 27, in Conroe ISD, per Conroe ISD’s 2026-27 calendar. Memorial Day is Monday, May 31. Summer camps should be almost full by now; if they’re not, the enrollment push started too late.

May is also the month to build every storm asset before you need it.

Start this month: hurricane-season pages, back-to-school creative for July and your Houston Restaurant Weeks application if you run a restaurant.

June and July 2027: hurricane season, Juneteenth and peak heat

Hurricane season runs June 1 to Nov. 30, and the historical peak is mid-September through October, per NOAA. If a storm heads for the Gulf Coast, your plan should already say which campaigns pause, which turn on and what you post. Our hurricane season marketing playbook walks through it phase by phase.

Juneteenth falls on Saturday, June 19. The holiday started in Galveston, and Houston’s celebrations in Emancipation Park and around the city draw big crowds. If your business takes part, show up with something real, like a sponsorship, a featured local vendor or an event. A logo with a flag on it doesn’t count. Father’s Day is the next day, Sunday, June 20.

July 4 is a Sunday. Expect most offices to close Monday, July 5, and many families to stretch it into a long weekend at Lake Conroe or Galveston.

July is peak heat, peak AC calls and the slowest month of the year for plenty of restaurants and retail. If you’re in the slow group, this is the month for a members-only offer to your email list rather than new-customer ads in a quiet market. If you’re an HVAC or pool company, keep the budget steady and answer the phone.

Start in June: the sales tax holiday offer and back-to-school ads. Start in July: Black Friday planning, honestly. Ten weeks out is not too early to pick the offer.

When does school start in Katy, Cy-Fair and Conroe ISD in 2027?

Katy ISD and Cy-Fair ISD both start the 2027-28 school year on Wednesday, Aug. 11, 2027, per Katy ISD’s adopted 2027-28 calendar and Cy-Fair ISD’s 2027-28 calendar (Cy-Fair’s was approved Nov. 9, 2026). Conroe ISD hadn’t published its 2027-28 calendar when we wrote this; it typically starts in the second week of August, and the 2026-27 year began Aug. 12.

The sales tax holiday runs Friday, Aug. 6 through Sunday, Aug. 8, per the Texas Comptroller. It falls the weekend before Katy and Cy-Fair start, so that weekend is the peak for clothing, shoes and school supplies in Katy, Cypress and the Tomball and FM 1960 corridors. If you sell qualifying items, your ads should run the week before, and they should say which items are tax-free. Vague “tax-free weekend sale” headlines are everywhere that week.

Back-to-school also means after-school enrollment: dance, martial arts, tutoring, music lessons and youth sports. Parents decide in late July, not the second week of school. Our back-to-school marketing post has more for The Woodlands specifically.

Restaurants: Houston Restaurant Weeks typically runs through August into Labor Day. In 2026 it ran Aug. 1 to Sept. 7 with nearly 400 restaurants, per Click2Houston. It’s a good way to get first-timers in the door during a slow month, as long as you plan how to bring them back.

September and October 2027: storm peak, fall breaks and holiday bookings

Labor Day is Monday, Sept. 6. After it, the calendar fills with Q4 decisions: holiday party bookings for venues and caterers, year-end appointments for medical and dental practices whose patients have met their deductibles, and the gift-card plan for anyone in retail or services.

It’s also the heart of hurricane season. Keep storm creative ready and keep a little budget in reserve. A storm in late September can wipe out a week of sales and then create a month of demand for roofers, tree services and restoration companies.

Fall breaks arrive in October: Katy ISD is out Oct. 8 to 12 and Cy-Fair Oct. 11 to 15, per the district calendars above. That’s a mini spring break for camps, family entertainment and travel. Halloween is a Sunday, which pushes parties and events to Friday and Saturday, Oct. 29 and 30.

October is when serious Black Friday work starts. Grow your email and SMS lists (with proper consent), decide on your offer and line up the creative. Our holiday marketing checklist is a useful way to make sure nothing is missing.

When should Houston businesses start Black Friday marketing?

Start planning the offer in September and start building your audience in early October. Warm-up ads and emails go out in the first two weeks of November, and the main push runs Thanksgiving week. For 2027, Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 25, Black Friday is Nov. 26, Small Business Saturday is Nov. 27 and Cyber Monday is Nov. 29.

Why so early? Ad prices on Meta and Google climb through November as the big retailers pile in. If your first contact with a shopper is a cold ad on Black Friday, you’re paying the year’s highest prices to reach someone who doesn’t know you. If they already saw your October content and joined your list, Black Friday is an email that costs you nothing to send.

A few other November dates. Daylight saving time ends Sunday, Nov. 7 (dark at 5:30 is bad news for evening foot traffic). Cy-Fair ISD is out the whole week of Nov. 22 to 26. And hurricane season ends Nov. 30, which is a good day to switch off storm ad groups you forgot about.

Small Business Saturday is the one to own if you’re a shop on Market Street, in Old Town Spring or downtown Conroe or Tomball. Lean into being local. The national chains can’t copy that.

December 2027: winter break, reviews and the 2028 plan

Cy-Fair ISD’s winter break runs Dec. 20 to 31, per Cy-Fair ISD’s 2027-28 calendar, and most districts are close. Christmas is a Saturday. For retail and restaurants it’s gift cards and last-minute shopping through Dec. 23. For most service businesses December is slow on new leads and busy on year-end jobs.

Use the slow part. Send review requests to everyone you served this fall while the job is fresh; a strong January on Google Maps starts in December. Clean up your tracking so every 2028 lead has a source. And draft next year’s calendar now, with the dates that worked this year marked and the ones that flopped crossed out.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Houston Rodeo in 2027?

RODEOHOUSTON runs from Tuesday, March 2 through Sunday, March 21, 2027, at NRG Park. The Downtown Rodeo Parade is Saturday, February 27. The nightly concert lineup is announced January 7 and individual tickets go on sale January 14. Businesses that want rodeo traffic should have offers and creative ready in early January, because most of the planning by customers happens before the gates open.

When is the Texas sales tax holiday in 2027?

The back-to-school sales tax holiday runs Friday, August 6 through Sunday, August 8, 2027, according to the Texas Comptroller. The emergency supplies holiday is typically the last weekend of April and the ENERGY STAR and water-efficient holidays typically fall on Memorial Day weekend, but those 2027 dates had not been posted when we wrote this. Check the Comptroller’s site before you advertise them.

When does school start in Cy-Fair ISD and Katy ISD in 2027?

Both Cy-Fair ISD and Katy ISD start the 2027-28 school year on Wednesday, August 11, 2027. Cy-Fair’s fall break is October 11 to 15 and Katy’s is October 8 to 12. Conroe ISD had not released its 2027-28 calendar at the time of writing; it typically starts in the second week of August. Back-to-school ads should start in early to mid July.

When is IRONMAN Texas 2027 in The Woodlands?

IRONMAN Texas is Saturday, April 24, 2027, and the race is set to stay in The Woodlands through 2030. Expect athletes and families to arrive midweek and road closures on race day around Lake Woodlands, the Hardy Toll Road and the Waterway. Nearby businesses should post race-day hours on Google and warn regular customers about closures a week ahead.

How far in advance should a small business plan holiday marketing in Houston?

Pick your holiday offer in September, start growing your email and text lists in October, and begin warm-up ads in early November. The main push runs Thanksgiving week, which in 2027 means Thanksgiving on November 25 through Cyber Monday on November 29. Ad costs rise through November, so reaching people before Black Friday is much cheaper than reaching them on it.
