The second week of December, a little after 9 p.m. The owner of a plumbing company in Spring has last year’s P&L open in one tab and a blank spreadsheet in the other. The 2026 “plan” was a sticky note: post more, boost a few things, maybe try those Google boxes. It went fine. March was a scramble, July was great, and October was quiet enough that he paid two techs to sit in the shop.

He doesn’t need a 40-page strategy deck. He needs to know what to spend each month, what it should produce, and which weeks in Houston will make or break the year.

That’s what this template is. A 2027 marketing plan for a Houston-area small business comes down to five decisions: an annual budget, a split across always-on, demand-capture and demand-creation work, a month-by-month calendar weighted to your real seasonality, four quarterly goals, and a short list of numbers you check every month. Everything below fits on one page when you’re done.

If you read our 2026 local marketing priorities, this is the next layer down: dates, dollars and the changes that landed in 2026. For how to pick the total number in the first place, start with how to set a marketing budget. We won’t repeat either here.

Step 1: split the budget before you pick channels

Most small business plans start with channels. “We should do TikTok.” Start with the money instead, and divide it into four buckets that do different jobs.

BucketWhat it pays forIllustrative shareCut it when
Base (always on)Website hosting and fixes, Google Business Profile upkeep, review requests, call tracking, CRM, email platform15% to 25%Never. This is the plumbing everything else runs through.
Capture (people already looking)Google Search ads, pay-per-lead (formerly Local Services Ads), local SEO, Microsoft Ads for some trades40% to 55%Cost per booked job rises for two straight months with no seasonal reason
Create (people not looking yet)Meta and Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, streaming TV, mailers, sponsorships15% to 30%You can’t staff the work capture is already sending
TestOne new channel or offer per quarter, with a stop date5% to 10%The stop date arrives and it hasn’t earned a second quarter

The shares are our starting point for a local service business, not a benchmark. A restaurant or boutique flips capture and create, because nobody searches “new brunch spot” the way they search “water heater repair Spring TX.” A CPA firm might put almost everything in capture and referrals.

Then spread the annual number across the months using a seasonality index. Give each month a weight based on your last two years of revenue or leads, make the 12 weights add up to 100, and multiply. A $60,000 year with July weighted at 11 gets $6,600 in July. January at 6 gets $3,600. Simple, and it stops you from spending evenly into a lopsided year.

One rule we hold to: spend ramps up two to four weeks before demand does. Google’s ad systems need a little time to settle after a budget jump, and your best-ranking competitors are already there when the first heat wave or the first freeze hits.

The Houston dates that shape 2027

Generic marketing calendars are built around national holidays. Houston has its own calendar, and three stretches of it matter more than any of those holidays for most local businesses.

Rodeo season. RODEOHOUSTON runs March 2 to March 21, 2027 at NRG Park, back to a 20-day format, per 93Q Country’s rundown of the 2027 dates. The Downtown Rodeo Parade is Saturday, February 27, per Click2Houston. For restaurants, bars, boot and western-wear shops, hotels near NRG and anyone selling to the visitors, that’s your Super Bowl. For many service businesses it’s a distraction: three weeks when half your customers’ evenings are spoken for.

Spring break lands inside the last week of rodeo. Conroe ISD’s break is March 15 to 19, 2027, per Conroe ISD’s 2026-27 calendar, and Cy-Fair ISD’s is the same week, per Community Impact on Cy-Fair ISD’s calendar. Check your own district (Katy, Klein, Tomball, Humble and Houston ISD set their own). That week, family-entertainment, tutoring camps, pet boarding and travel-adjacent businesses get busy, and appointment-based businesses see no-shows climb.

Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, per NOAA. Restoration, roofing, tree service, generator installers and insurance agents should have storm pages and paused ad groups built in May, ready to switch on the same afternoon. Everyone else needs a plan for the opposite: pausing promotions, swapping creative and posting real hours when the power’s out.

Smaller dates worth putting on the plan now: the Chevron Houston Marathon on January 17, 2027 (ABC13 expects about 40,000 participants across race weekend), the last day of school on May 27 in both Conroe ISD and Cy-Fair ISD, Texas’ sales tax holiday from August 6 to 8, 2027, according to the Texas Comptroller, and Cy-Fair’s first day of the 2027-28 school year on August 11, a calendar approved in November 2026. The state also runs an emergency-supplies tax holiday each spring; it was April 25 to 27 in 2026, so watch for the 2027 announcement if you sell generators, batteries or storm gear.

Quarterly goals that actually steer the year

Monthly tasks keep you busy. Quarterly goals tell you whether the busy-ness is working. Write one outcome per quarter, with a number attached, and one “fix” project that makes the next quarter cheaper.

What changed going into 2027

Four shifts from 2026 belong in your plan. We’ve kept this to what we could verify.

Google’s search ads are moving into AI answers. On May 20, 2026, Google said it was testing new ad formats inside AI Mode, including ads that answer a specific question and “Highlighted Answers” that can appear in AI Mode’s recommendation lists, per Google’s May 2026 announcement. According to PPC Land’s Google Marketing Live recap, those placements require AI Max for Search or Performance Max, and Dynamic Search Ads were set to upgrade to AI Max automatically in September 2026. For a small business, the practical move is to keep a well-run Search campaign with good conversion tracking, and to make sure your website plainly answers the questions customers ask. We wrote about the organic side in AI Overviews and local search and showing up in AI search.

Local Services Ads now live inside Google Ads. Starting in August 2026, Google began moving LSA accounts into Google Ads as Performance Max campaigns with pay-per-lead goals, with later phases in late 2026 and 2027, per Google’s transition guide. Manual bidding is gone, budgets are daily instead of weekly, and old performance reports don’t transfer. Export yours before your migration date. Budget lines in your plan that said “LSA” now sit inside Google Ads, which makes it easier to compare cost per lead side by side with regular search.

Cookies stayed, but measurement still runs on your own data. Google decided in April 2025 to keep third-party cookies in Chrome, then in October 2025 retired most Privacy Sandbox ad technologies, per PPC Land. That removed a deadline without fixing the underlying problem: browsers, phones and consent banners still drop a lot of tracking. Plan for first-party measurement: tracked phone numbers, forms that pass the lead source into your CRM, and offline conversion uploads. Our GA4 and Google Ads tracking guide walks through the setup.

Texas privacy and texting rules apply to more of your marketing than you think. The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act took effect July 1, 2024. Small businesses as defined by the SBA are generally exempt, but even a small business must get consent before selling a consumer’s sensitive data, per the Texas Attorney General. Separately, SB 140 brought marketing text messages under Texas’ telephone solicitation law as of September 1, 2025, with exemptions that may or may not cover you, per Morgan Lewis’s summary of SB 140. If texting past customers is in your Q3 retention plan, get clear opt-in and ask your attorney before launch. Our SMS marketing rules post has more.

Mistakes we see in small business plans

  • Even monthly budgets. A pool company spending the same in January and June is overpaying in one and starving the other.
  • No stop dates on tests. A channel tried “for a while” never gets judged. Put the end date in the plan when you start it.
  • Planning around national holidays only. In Houston, rodeo, spring break and storm season move more money than Presidents’ Day does.
  • Lead counts as the scoreboard. A month with 120 leads and 9 jobs is worse than a month with 40 leads and 15 jobs. Track all the way to booked work.
  • No room for a storm. Every Houston plan needs a line for what happens if a hurricane, a freeze or a long outage hits: what pauses, what turns on and who posts updates.
  • Writing the plan once. Re-forecast at the end of each quarter. A plan that doesn’t change after real data comes in is a wish list.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a marketing plan for a small business in 2027?

Start with an annual budget, then split it into always-on basics, demand capture like search ads, demand creation like social ads, and a small test budget. Spread it across the months using your own seasonality, set one measurable goal per quarter, and choose five or six KPIs to check monthly. For a Houston business, add local dates like rodeo season, spring break and hurricane season.

When is RODEOHOUSTON in 2027?

The 2027 Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo runs March 2 through March 21 at NRG Park, returning to a 20-day format. The Downtown Rodeo Parade is Saturday, February 27, 2027. Several large districts, including Conroe ISD and Cy-Fair ISD, have spring break March 15 to 19, which falls inside the rodeo’s final week, so plan family-focused promotions and staffing around that overlap.

How should a small business split its marketing budget?

One practical method is four buckets: 15% to 25% for always-on basics like your website, Google Business Profile, reviews and tracking; 40% to 55% for capturing people already searching; 15% to 30% for reaching people not yet looking; and 5% to 10% for one test per quarter. Those shares are a starting point for local service businesses. Adjust them for your own industry and results.

What marketing changes should small businesses plan for in 2027?

Google began moving Local Services Ads into Google Ads as pay-per-lead Performance Max campaigns in August 2026, with more accounts following into 2027. Google is also testing ads inside AI Mode answers. Chrome kept third-party cookies but tracking gaps remain, so first-party measurement matters. Texas privacy and texting rules, including SB 140 for marketing texts, also deserve a check with your attorney.

When is the Texas sales tax holiday in 2027?

According to the Texas Comptroller, the 2027 sales tax holiday runs from Friday, August 6 through midnight Sunday, August 8. Qualifying clothing, footwear, school supplies and backpacks under the price limit are exempt. Retailers and businesses selling to families should plan back-to-school promotions and ad budgets around that weekend, along with local school start dates in the second week of August.
